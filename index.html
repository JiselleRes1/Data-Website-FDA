About this data

Source
Full TMDB Movies Dataset (asaniczka, Kaggle), downloaded 2026-09-27. Poster art in the marquee above is served live from TMDB's public image CDN.
What one row is
One row is one movie with a "Released" status on TMDB, identified by its release date and its primary genre.
Rows dropped and why
From ~1.5 million raw rows: kept only status == "Released"; dropped rows with an unparseable release date or a release year outside 1950–2026; dropped rows with no listed genre (took the first listed genre as each movie's primary_genre); dropped rows with fewer than 10 TMDB votes. Result: 72,775 rows. See scripts/clean_data.py for the exact code.
How rates and averages are computed
Yield = revenue ÷ budget, computed only for movies with both budget > 0 and revenue > 0 reported (8,645 of 72,775 movies). Genre-level yield uses the median to avoid outliers dominating, and is only reported for genres with at least 30 financially-reported titles. All other averages are means over the full 72,775-row dataset unless stated otherwise.
